# Legacy Signal

Legacy Signal treats achievement as recorded evidence rather than decoration. Space is divided with editorial conviction: one dominant result, identity, or portrait establishes the meaning, while supporting data follows in measured bands and precise alignments. Every composition should feel meticulously crafted, as if its hierarchy was tuned through countless small optical corrections by a sports design team at the top of its field.

Near-black is the material base, purple is atmosphere, and metallic gold is reserved for the fact that matters most. Color never fills space merely to create excitement; it certifies rank, selection, and consequence. Phoenix geometry appears as cropped facets, directional cuts, or a quiet seal—not as a large illustrative mascot competing with the record. The balance must show painstaking restraint and deep expertise.

Scale creates rhythm. A placement, player portrait, performance trajectory, or standings table becomes the visual anchor for its respective composition. Secondary numbers are grouped by meaning instead of repeated in equal boxes. Typography is sparse and functional: Orbitron gives tournament names, ranks, and championship values their voice, while Raleway carries every label and explanatory detail with calm clarity.

The family is unified through shared margins, disciplined neutral surfaces, subtle image treatment, and a consistent AEVIC signature. It is differentiated through composition: ceremonial asymmetry for a result, analytical movement for performance, portrait-led certification for MVP, and rigorous rows for standings. Each design must look labored over with master-level execution rather than generated from one reusable poster template.

The final work communicates through proportion, contrast, and spatial tension before it asks the viewer to read. Every edge, crop, numeral, and interval receives painstaking attention. Long names, missing imagery, and dense data are not exceptions hidden by decoration; they are proof that the system was meticulously crafted to remain authoritative under real competitive conditions.
